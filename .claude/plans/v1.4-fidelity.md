## 1. Frequency response

### 1.1 The "light between 40 Hz and 2 kHz" is a level-match artefact, not a tone error

Raw (null-gain-matched) medians on the clean sweep read as a broad ~−0.9 dB midband deficit,
which is what prompted the observation. Re-normalising at 1 kHz collapses it:

| Band | raw delta (clean) | **1 kHz-normalised (clean)** |
|---|---|---|
| 101 Hz | −0.56 | +0.61 |
| 254 Hz | −0.84 | +0.08 |
| 508 Hz | −0.94 | −0.04 |
| 1016 Hz | −0.85 | 0.00 |
| 2032 Hz | −0.91 | +0.11 |

Normalised, **every band from 200 Hz to 5 kHz is within ±0.35 dB at every one of the four sweep
levels** (median across all 16 captures). There is no midband error to fix.

The mechanism: `null_depth()` fits one broadband gain by least squares. The genuine LF excess
(§1.2) drags that fitted gain *down*, and the deficit reappears spread across the midband. It is
**one error, not two.**

> **Harness note:** this is a real trap in how the report reads. See work item **W5**.

### 1.2 REAL — low-frequency excess below ~100 Hz, and it is clipping-mediated

1 kHz-normalised median across all 16 captures (dB, plugin − pedal):

| Band | clean (−30) | drv −18 | drv −12 | drv −6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 Hz | **+2.78** | +1.56 | +1.33 | +1.26 |
| 25 Hz | +2.53 | +1.43 | +1.21 | +1.15 |
| 32 Hz | +2.01 | +1.13 | +0.88 | +0.81 |
| 40 Hz | +1.42 | +0.76 | +0.55 | +0.48 |
| 64 Hz | +0.90 | +0.26 | +0.23 | +0.14 |
| 101 Hz | +0.61 | +0.07 | +0.08 | +0.00 |

Positive in **16/16 captures at 20 Hz**. So the observation holds — but the cause is *not* a
mis-set high-pass corner, because:

**(a) It is clip-mode dependent.** Clean sweep, 1 kHz-normalised, median per mode:

| Band | Hard | Medium | Soft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 Hz | +2.78 | **+3.93** | +1.89 |
| 40 Hz | +1.40 | **+2.13** | +0.81 |
| 80 Hz | +0.55 | **+0.98** | +0.29 |

A *linear* filter error cannot depend on which diodes are in circuit. At matched drive the
ordering is consistent — e.g. at D0.50 B0.50, 40 Hz: Medium +2.26, Hard +1.25, Soft +0.55.

**(b) It shrinks monotonically as the clipper is driven harder** (+2.78 → +1.26 at 20 Hz), which
is what compression against a clip ceiling does to any level difference.

**(c) The single least-driven capture inverts the sign.** `Hard D0.20 B0.50` on the clean sweep
is −0.87 dB at 40 Hz and −1.51 dB at 80 Hz — i.e. LF *shy*, not hot. Every other capture is
D0.35 or above.

**Reading:** the plugin's Stage-1 BASS boost engages harder relative to the pedal's as DRIVE
rises — a **BASS ↔ DRIVE coupling error in the shared Stage-1 feedback network**, which is
exactly the coupling `circuit.md` warns must not be decoupled and which `CLAUDE.md`'s "B0.65
SHAPE fails" residual already flags as *confounded*. This data confirms it is real and quantifies
it, but **cannot separate it from the underlying linear BASS taper**, because in `pedal2` BASS and
DRIVE co-vary (B0.50 only ever appears with D0.20–0.50; B0.65 only with D0.65–1.00).

> This is not a capture-chain LF-rolloff artefact: there is no bypass anchor in `pedal2` to rule
> that out directly, but a fixed chain high-pass would bias *every* capture positive, and
> `Hard D0.20` is negative.

### 1.3 REAL — top octave is 2–3 dB shy at high DRIVE and high input level

1 kHz-normalised, `sweep_drv_-6`, per capture (dB):

| Capture | 6.4 kHz | 8.1 kHz | 10.2 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hard D0.20 | +0.62 | +0.49 | −0.17 |
| Hard D0.35 | +1.09 | +0.36 | +0.12 |
| Hard D0.50 | +0.39 | +0.12 | −0.71 |
| Hard D0.65 | −0.84 | −1.04 | −1.29 |
| Hard D0.80 | −1.15 | −1.72 | **−2.78** |
| Hard D1.00 | −0.98 | −1.78 | **−3.02** |
| Soft D1.00 | −0.87 | −1.99 | −2.02 |
| Medium D1.00 | −1.03 | −1.80 | −2.74 |

Clip-mode independent; onset is sharply at **DRIVE ≥ 0.65**, and it grows with input level
(median 10 kHz: −0.65 at −18, −0.75 at −12, −1.43 at −6). At the clean sweep it is only
−0.2…−0.8 dB.

**Why this is newly actionable:** v1.2 tried a high-drive HF shelf and reverted it, on the reading
that "the high-drive gap is a flat broadband LEVEL deficit, not a tilt". **That premise no longer
holds** — v1.2.1's `kIs` fix closed the LEVEL deficit (pedal2 LEVEL 12/16 → 16/16). What remains
after that fix is a genuine *tilt*, visible here for the first time on level-normalised per-band
data. `DriveTilt` deliberately fades to zero by ~G0.8, so nothing in the chain currently
compensates this region.

Candidate mechanisms, in order of plausibility:
1. **C1 (100p) across the Stage-1 feedback.** At DRIVE→max the feedback leg is ~1 MΩ, putting the
   C1 pole at ~1.6 kHz. The plugin's top-octave loss switching on at exactly D0.65 points here.
   Check the *effective* feedback impedance the model presents vs. the DRIVE taper's top end.
2. **DRIVE taper top end** (`1e6·x^2.2`) — if the modelled resistance at x≥0.65 is too high, C1
   rolls off too early. Coupled with (1).
3. Rail-clip ADAA smoothing of genuine high-order harmonics (should be tiny at 8x — verify, don't
   assume).

## 2. THD and harmonics

### 2.1 REAL and isolated — Medium mode is the only clip mode with a level-dependent THD error

THD delta = `20·log10(plugin% / pedal%)`, median over 100 Hz–2 kHz Farina bands:

| Mode | −18 dBFS | −12 dBFS | −6 dBFS |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soft** | +0.26 | +0.22 | +0.28 |
| **Hard** | +0.27 | −0.27 | −0.71 |
| **Medium** | −0.18 | −0.95 | **−1.88** |

Absolute, median 100 Hz–2 kHz at −6 dBFS: Medium **pedal 15.72% vs plugin 13.31%**.
Per capture, every Medium setting from D0.50 up lands −1.7 to −2.2 dB. **Soft is essentially
exact at every level and drive** — which is what makes Medium separable: the shared diode
parameters are pinned by Soft.

Harmonic breakdown (median over 110/220/440 Hz anchors, `sweep_drv_-6`), odd orders only — the
ones that carry the sound:

| Mode | H3 | H5 | H7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft | +0.28 | +0.29 | +0.52 |
| Hard | −0.64 | −0.20 | −0.16 |
| Medium | **−1.97** | **−1.79** | **−1.61** |

Medium is down **uniformly across H3/H5/H7**, i.e. it is producing *less clipping*, not a
different harmonic *shape*. This is a clip-threshold/knee error, not a spectral-tilt error.

### 2.2 REAL — clip onset is far too early at low DRIVE  ·  ✅ FIXED (2026-07-27, W2)

> The numbers below are the PRE-FIX state and are kept as the problem statement. After W2's DRIVE
> taper re-fit the same capture reads **1.68 / 7.34 / 10.17 %** at 101 Hz (median 100 Hz–2 kHz error
> +0.40 / −0.03 / −0.70 dB). See W2's OUTCOME block — and note the diagnosis in the last paragraph
> here, that this is a diode/`kIs` tension, turned out to be **wrong**: it is the pre-clip gain law.

`Hard D0.20 B0.50 T0.20`, THD% pedal/plugin (Farina):

| Band | −18 dBFS | −12 dBFS | −6 dBFS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 Hz | **2.55 / 11.28** | 9.34 / 14.49 | 12.49 / 14.26 |
| 403 Hz | 3.51 / 13.34 | 9.59 / 14.62 | 11.39 / 13.22 |
| 1016 Hz | 6.01 / 13.40 | 9.20 / 12.53 | 8.67 / 10.19 |

At −18 dBFS the plugin distorts **~4.4×** as much as the pedal, broadband. The error collapses as
level rises (+11.0 dB → +3.4 → +1.3). This is the *edge-of-breakup* region — the most musically
exposed part of the pedal — and it is the largest single error in the whole dataset.

Note the tension with v1.2.1: halving `kIs` lowers the diode threshold, i.e. it moves clip onset
*earlier*, which is the wrong direction for this. `CLAUDE.md` already records that a steeper `kIs`
reduction broke `ClippingStage_Sine`'s large-signal Hard check — the two constraints pull opposite
ways under a single global `kIs`.

⚠️ **n = 1 capture.** `D0.20` appears once, Hard only. Real, but under-sampled.

### 2.3 REAL — the modelled Soft↔Medium threshold gap is far too small

At `D0.35 B0.50`, THD% at 101 Hz, −18 dBFS:

| Mode | pedal | plugin |
|---|---|---|
| Soft | 18.13 | 18.82 |
| Medium | **12.43** | **19.02** |
| Hard | 17.86 | 21.03 |

The **real pedal's Medium position is markedly cleaner than its Soft position** at this setting.
The model has them within 0.2%. That follows directly from how the modes are built in
`src/dsp/Stage1.h`:

```
Soft   -> diodePair.setParams(2.0 * kIs, ...)   // two antiparallel pairs in parallel
Medium -> diodePair.setParams(      kIs, ...)   // one antiparallel pair
```

A 2× ratio in `Is` is worth only `n·Vt·ln(2)` = 45.3 mV × 0.693 ≈ **31 mV** of threshold — far too
small to produce the pedal's observed gap. Either the real SW1 "mid" position is not simply
"one pair instead of two" (a series element / extra diode drop / series resistor in that leg), or
the Soft/Medium branch needs an independent parameter. **This wants a `schematic-checker` pass
before any DSP change.**

At D0.50 and above all three modes agree well (pedal 22.0–23.7 vs plugin 22.6–24.1) — the
threshold difference washes out once everything is saturating, which is why this was never caught.

### 2.4 NOT actionable — the huge even-harmonic deltas in the executive summary

`executive_summary.txt` §4 reports H2 deltas of +12 to +25 dB for Soft/Medium and a
"median |H-delta|" of 3.2 dB for Medium. Those are floor comparisons. Absolute levels at
`sweep_drv_-6` (median over anchors, dBc):

| Mode | H2 pedal / plugin | H4 pedal / plugin | H6 pedal / plugin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft | −52.1 / −50.7 | −54.7 / −56.8 | −58.6 / −60.8 |
| Medium | −48.8 / −50.7 | −54.9 / −56.2 | −61.9 / −59.6 |

Symmetric clippers have no even harmonics in either the pedal or the plugin; at −50…−62 dBc the
dB *ratio* is dominated by the capture noise floor and is meaningless. The audit's harmonic median
should exclude harmonics below an absolute floor. See work item **W5**.

### 2.5 FLAGGED, needs validation — THD at 6.4/8.1 kHz

Median across captures at `sweep_drv_-6`: pedal 1.49% / 2.16%, plugin **0.11% / 0.12%** — a 10–20×
gap. Directionally consistent with §1.3 (plugin's top octave short of energy at high level), but
these two bands sit near the Farina ceiling with only **order 2** measurable, and their H2 lands at
13–16 kHz where both the pedal's own C5/C11 filters and the capture chain roll off hard. Treat as
*unvalidated* until §1.3 is understood — do not fit anything to it.

### W2 — Low-drive clip onset  ·  ✅ FIXED (2026-07-27)

> **OUTCOME: the lever was the DRIVE TAPER, not the diodes.** `TaperUtils.h`'s `driveResistance`
> exponent **2.2 → 2.75**, plus a jointly re-fitted `DriveTilt::kMaxGainDB` **2.5 → 1.0 dB**.
> Reproduce with `analysis/.venv/bin/python3 analysis/w2_clip_onset.py --only onset,levers,fit`.
> Full derivations live in those two files' comments; this block records the argument and the cost.

**Result.** The target error is gone: `Hard D0.20`'s median 100 Hz–2 kHz THD error goes
**+11.61 / +3.67 / +1.26 → +0.40 / −0.03 / −0.70 dB** at −18/−12/−6 dBFS. Across all 16 captures ×
3 depths, THD error **rms 1.88 → 0.52 dB, worst 11.61 → 1.14 dB**. `knob_tracking.py` (SIGNAL=v2,
pedal2): **THD 15/16 → 16/16** (G0.20 was the only THD failure in the set), **LEVEL 14/16 → 15/16**,
SHAPE 13/16 → 12/16. `ctest` 10/10, `auval` passes.

**Why the taper and not the diodes** — the three findings, in the order they land:

1. **The error is mode-INDEPENDENT in trend.** At −18 dBFS it is monotone in DRIVE and present in
   every clip mode (D0.35: Soft +1.0 / Hard +1.4 / Medium +2.8 dB; D ≥ 0.50 within ±0.7; D0.20
   +11.6). Diode parameters are per-mode; a shared pre-clip gain error is not. The per-mode ordering
   at D0.35 follows each mode's *overdrive margin* (probe 2), not its diode parameters — Medium has
   the least margin and the largest error.
2. **`kAsymMismatch` is refuted as the lever** (probe 3a, six Hard captures × 3 depths). It moves H2
   and essentially nothing else: dTHD at D ≥ 0.35 is 0.57–0.62 dB rms for *every* value from 0 to
   0.45, and dLEVEL is invariant to it. Driving it to 0 does cut D0.20/−18 from +11.6 to +5.4 dB,
   but it destroys the even-harmonic match that holds at every other drive (rms dH2 1.34 →
   unmeasurable). No single value satisfies both. Hard's H2 already matches within ~1.8 dB at every
   drive from 0.35 up, so the asymmetry was never the thing that was wrong — its +13.6 dB blow-up at
   D0.20/−18 is a *consequence* of the model being past onset where the pedal is not.
3. **The clip-onset margin table explains why only this corner is observable** (probe 2, closed
   form). Stage 1 decomposes exactly: the op-amp holds node_C at Vin, so `ig = Vin/Zg` is set by
   BASS and input level *alone* — independent of DRIVE — while `Vf = Vin·|Zf/Zg|` carries all the
   DRIVE dependence, and the clamp sits at `n·Vt_eff·ln(ig/Is)` at every DRIVE setting. Result: the
   whole dataset sits **8–40 dB past onset except `Hard D0.20`/−18 at +5.4 dB**. Pre-clip gain is
   observable *only* there; everywhere else THD has saturated and is blind to it. That is both why
   the 2.2 fit missed this and why correcting it costs nothing at D ≥ 0.35.

**Why the n=1 objection above does not apply.** The old plan text worried that a `D0.20`-only fit
would trade a measured error for an unmeasured one, and required the change to leave D ≥ 0.35
bit-identical. It does better than that: D ≥ 0.35 THD rms **improves 0.71 → 0.45 dB**, and the
evidence is **four** captures (Hard D0.20 plus D0.35 in all three modes), not one. The 2.2 exponent
also predates both v1.2.1's `kIs` halving and W1's Medium threshold — it was matching mid-drive THD
through a clipper that has since changed twice, and mid drive is exactly where THD is blind to gain.
x = 1 is unchanged at 1 MΩ, so full drive is bit-identical.

**Joint THD + FR fit (probe 4).** A pre-clip gain law does not only move THD — at low drive it also
moves how hard the clipper compresses, hence the measured FR. Scored over all 16 captures × 4 sweep
depths, reproducing knob_tracking's gates exactly (verified: at the old 2.2 the probe returns SHAPE
13/16 and LEVEL 14/16, matching the tool):

| exp/tilt | THD all | THD D≤.20 | THD D≥.35 | FR clean | FR −18 | FR −12 | FR −6 | SHAPE | LEVEL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2 / 2.5 (was) | 1.90 | 7.10 | 0.71 | 0.546 | 0.339 | 0.360 | 0.363 | 13/16 | 14/16 |
| 2.6 / 1.0 | 0.84 | 2.92 | 0.43 | 0.592 | 0.235 | 0.213 | 0.275 | 13/16 | 15/16 |
| **2.75 / 1.0** | **0.50** | **0.62** | **0.49** | 0.620 | **0.247** | **0.229** | **0.275** | 12/16 | 15/16 |
| 2.85 / 1.0 | 0.59 | 1.15 | 0.54 | 0.631 | 0.253 | 0.235 | 0.275 | 12/16 | 15/16 |

**`DriveTilt` had to be re-fitted, not assumed.** It is fitted against the *same* low-drive captures
the taper is, so the taper re-fit invalidated its 2.5 dB by construction. Re-deriving gives 1.0 dB,
and the finding is worth keeping: 2.5 dB was partly compensating **clipping compression**, not the
linear tilt the shelf is for — the old taper over-drove low DRIVE, the clipper squashed the top
